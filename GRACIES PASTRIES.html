Prep Time Bake Time Yield

Instructions

  1. Cream the butter and sugars until fluffy.

Gooey Cinnamon Rolls

The ultimate comfort pastry. The secret is the swirl and the thick cream cheese glaze.

What You'll Need

Dough
Warm milk, yeast, flour, sugar, and butter.
Filling
Ground cinnamon, brown sugar, and melted butter.
Glaze
Cream cheese, powdered sugar, and a splash of vanilla.

The Swirl Method

The secret to perfect rolls is the tightness of the roll. Ensure you roll the dough as tightly as possible before cutting with floss to avoid squishing the layers.
